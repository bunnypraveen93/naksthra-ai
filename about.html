About Us

Nakshatra AI is a smart AI assistant app for Android. It was created by Bunny Praveen, an independent developer from India, to make everyday phone tasks easier through natural conversation.

What the app does

You can chat with the assistant by typing or by voice, ask questions that need fresh information from the web, and run simple commands on your phone such as making calls or opening apps. Reminders and notes help you manage daily work.

Why we built it

Many assistants are hard to use in regional contexts and are cluttered with unnecessary features. Our goal is a fast, simple assistant that feels natural to talk to.

Our promise

We keep the app free to download, explain every permission it asks for, and respond to user feedback. Questions? Visit our Contact page.
