How to Install

Since the app is currently provided as an APK, Google Play Protect might block the installation. Follow these steps to install:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on your device.
  2. Tap on your profile icon and select Play Protect.
  3. Tap the Settings (gear) icon and temporarily turn off "Scan apps with Play Protect".
  4. Install the Nakshatra AI APK.
  5. Once installed, turn Play Protect back on for your safety.

Note: We are working hard to bring Nakshatra AI directly to the Google Play Store very soon!
