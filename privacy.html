Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how Nakshatra AI (the Android app and this website) handles information. By using them you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not run user accounts on this website and do not ask you to submit personal details here. Messages you send to the AI assistant are processed by third-party AI service providers to generate a reply. Please do not share passwords, bank details or other sensitive information in chats. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use your conversations to train our own AI models.

2. App permissions

Accessibility service: used only to perform commands you ask for on your device, such as opening apps or tapping on screen. Phone and contacts: used only to place calls or find a contact when you ask. Microphone: used only for voice mode. The app does not use these permissions in the background to collect or upload your personal data. You can revoke any permission in your phone settings at any time.

This website uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, or visit aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Log data

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may automatically record technical data such as IP address, browser type and visit time for security and performance.

5. Third-party links

Our site links to services such as GitHub, where the APK is hosted. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites.

6. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
